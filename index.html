How to use this calculator 🌟

  1. Tap the date box and choose your date of birth.
  2. Press the “Find My Birth Day ✨” button.
  3. See the weekday you were born on, plus your age, zodiac sign and birthday countdown!

Frequently Asked Questions 💭

How does this calculator know what day I was born?

It uses the calendar built into your device. Every date in history matches exactly one weekday, so the calculator just looks it up for you in a second.

Is my birth date saved anywhere?

No! Everything happens inside your own browser. Your date is never sent or stored anywhere.

What if I was born on February 29?

Lucky you, a leap-day baby! 🐸 In years without February 29, we count your birthday on February 28.
